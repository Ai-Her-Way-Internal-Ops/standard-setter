# Fictional deck: create → check → revise
This is a text-only teaching fixture. It is not a real speaker deck or a visual-quality test.

## Brief and sources
Audience: small studio owners. Objective: explain a two-step project intake process.
S1 (synthetic policy v1): “Step one is the discovery call. Step two is a written scope approved by the client. Delivery timing is agreed after scope approval.”
S2 (synthetic CTA instruction v1): “Close by inviting the audience to write down one process they will clarify this week.”

Text rubric: T1 claims match S1; T2 both steps appear in order; T3 close matches S2.

## Worker draft v1
Slide 1: Every project is delivered in seven days.
Slide 2: Start with a discovery call.
Slide 3: Book our paid programme today.

## Expected review
FAIL. T1: unsupported seven-day promise. T2: missing scope approval. T3: wrong close. Return each exact location and correction to the worker.

## Worker revision v2
Slide 1: A clear start for your next project.
Slide 2: First, hold a discovery call. Then prepare a written scope for the client to approve. Agree delivery timing after scope approval. [S1]
Slide 3: Write down one process you will clarify this week. [S2]

## Expected re-review
PASS on T1–T3 after independently comparing v2 with S1 and S2. It remains unreviewed for visual layout until rendered slides are supplied and inspected. If visual review is required in the brief, the overall verdict is BLOCKED until that inspection occurs.
